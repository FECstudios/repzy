About meal photos

The photo is downscaled on your device and sent to an AI provider for analysis. We do not store your photo on our servers — only the resulting food name and nutrition values are saved to your diary. We do not request camera permission: your device's own camera app takes the picture.

Health data and explicit consent

Body measurements and health metrics are treated as sensitive personal data under Turkish data-protection law (KVKK Art. 6) and special category data under the GDPR. We process them only on the basis of the explicit consent you give when you first open the app. To withdraw consent, delete your account — all of your data is deleted with it.

3. What we do not collect

  • Location data
  • Contacts, call logs, SMS
  • Advertising identifiers (the app has no ads)
  • Third-party analytics or tracking SDKs
  • Other photos in your gallery — we only access the photo you pick

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising. We use only the service providers (processors) needed to run the app:

These providers may host servers outside your country, so your data may be transferred internationally. Your explicit consent covers this transfer.

5. Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, all records (profile, measurements, workouts, food, water, coaching history) are removed from the database immediately; removal from technical backups takes up to 30 days.

6. Deleting your account and data

In the app go to Settings → Delete my account. If you have already uninstalled the app, you can request deletion from this page.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected or deleted, and to object to processing. You can correct your profile and measurements yourself in the app's Settings screen. For anything else, write to privacy@repzy.app — we respond within 30 days.

8. Security

Connections are encrypted with TLS. Row-level security is enabled in the database, so each user can only read their own rows. The AI provider's API key is never shipped inside the app; it exists only on the server.

9. Children

Repzy is not directed to users under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe we hold data belonging to someone under 16, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Medical disclaimer

Repzy is a wellness and educational app. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any disease. Calculations and suggestions are estimates; consult a healthcare professional before changing your training or nutrition.

11. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date on this page and notify you in the app for significant changes.
