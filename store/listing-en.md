# Play Store listing — English (primary market)

Title (30 char max): **Repzy: AI Fitness & Nutrition**

## Short description (80 char max)

AI fitness & nutrition coach that adapts to your goals, level and progress.

## Full description (4000 char max)

Starting from zero at the gym is the hard part. Repzy is the coach that
figures out what you should do today — and adjusts as you get stronger.

Most fitness apps are either a workout log or a calorie counter. Repzy is
both, connected: your training and your nutrition feed the same coach, so
the advice you get is built on your actual data, not a generic template.

WHAT REPZY DOES

🏋️ A daily plan, already built
Tell Repzy your goal, experience level and equipment once. Every day it
builds a workout matched to that — full body for beginners, upper/lower/core
splits for advanced lifters — and adjusts automatically if you've already
trained enough this week.

📸 Snap your meal, skip the typing
Take a photo of your food and Repzy estimates calories and macros in
seconds. No manual search, no barcode hunting.

🤖 A coach that actually reads your data
Once a day, Repzy looks at your recent workouts, meals, water and weight
trend and writes you a short, specific brief — not a canned tip, a reaction
to what you actually did yesterday.

💪 Every movement, explained properly
16 core exercises with step-by-step form cues, common mistakes to avoid, and
a gym-or-home alternative for each one. Filter by muscle group, equipment
and experience level.

📊 Progress you can see
Calories eaten vs. target, macros, a 7-day calorie chart, weight history and
BMI — all on one screen, updated the moment you log something.

💧 Water and streaks
Quick-add buttons for water, a daily streak that counts any day you log a
workout, a meal or water, and reminders you control.

🖼️ Private progress photos
Optional front/side/back photos to compare your own transformation over
time — stored privately, never sent to any AI, deletable any time.

🌍 Built multilingual
Full English, Turkish and Russian interfaces, matched to your device
language automatically.

WHY REPZY IS DIFFERENT

Exercise-reference apps are dictionaries — useful, but static. Repzy is a
coach: it remembers who you are, builds a plan around that, and changes its
advice as your data changes. The exercise library is one part of it, not
the whole product.

PRIVACY

Your body measurements and health data are only ever processed with your
explicit, separate consent. Meal photos are analyzed and discarded — never
stored. Progress photos are private, encrypted at rest, and never touched
by AI. You can delete your account and every record in one tap, right from
the app.

Repzy is a wellness and education app. It does not diagnose, treat or
prevent any condition. Talk to a healthcare professional before changing
your training or nutrition, especially if you have an existing condition.
